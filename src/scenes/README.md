# src/scenes — Phaser scenes (presentation and input only; rules live in src/logic/)

| Scene (key) | File | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Boot` | `boot-scene.ts` | Loads and validates the content JSON; looks for the sprite atlas; then Preload, or Error. |
| `Preload` | `preload-scene.ts` | Loads every asset in the data (also the title image, the intro coworker and the jingle), every map tileset image and the player sheet (the sprites in the atlas come from its one image and are copied to textures under their own paths); shows the loading bar. When done: launches Hud and Debug when enabled, emits `game:ready` and starts Title. |
| `Title` | `title-scene.ts` | The title: `intro.json › titleImage` (the pixel office), the logo and a blinking "Pulsa START". Start / A / tap → menu: *Continuar* (only with a valid `flexymon.save.v1`; a broken save was copied to `flexymon.save.broken` and only *Nueva partida* shows, with "No se pudo leer la partida guardada.") and *Nueva partida*; B back to "Pulsa START". Continuar → the saved game in the Overworld. Nueva partida over a save asks "¿Empezar una nueva?" Sí / No (cursor on No) → Name. `quickStart()` is for the E2E hooks only. |
| `Name` | `name-scene.ts` | Name entry: letter grid (A–Z, Ñ, á é í ó ú from `name.letters`, space, Borrar, OK); arrows + A, B deletes (on an empty name: back to Title), Start = OK, taps/clicks press cells. Turns on the Input service's text mode: letter keys (Z, X, WASD, M too), Space and Backspace type. Max 10 characters; OK with no letter says "Escribe al menos una letra.". Rules: `src/logic/name-entry.ts`. Then Intro with `{ name }`. |
| `Intro` | `intro-scene.ts` | The new-game intro, scripted by `intro.json` on the `start.json › spawn` map (drawn with `drawZoneMap`): `fade_in`, `wait`, `walk` (player or coworker, one tile per direction), `face`, `say` (coworker lines, `{name}` / `{starter}`), `handover` (jingle, "¡Recibes a {starter}!", then the secret-name items and message). The new game comes from `start.json` (`newGameState`); at the end the player's tile goes into the session and the Overworld starts there. Rules: `src/logic/intro.ts`. |
| `Error` | `error-scene.ts` | Red list of content/map errors; scroll with up/down (Input service), wheel or drag. |
| `Overworld` | `overworld-scene.ts` | Draws a zone's map (loaded at boot) and moves the player on the grid; camera follows, clamped (interiors centred on black). Warps: lock, fade out 200 ms, restart on the target, fade in, unlock. Start with `{ map, x?, y?, facing?, fadeIn? }`. Emits `zone:entered`, `player:moved`; listens to `debug:teleport`, `debug:menu`, `debug:battle`, `battle:ended`. Battles: `startBattle()` flashes 600 ms, launches Battle and sleeps; on `battle:ended` it wakes on the same tile, fades in and resets safe steps. NPCs: spawns the zone's NPCs, which wander and block; A facing an NPC → it turns to the player and its lines show in the DialogBox, the player frozen until the last box. Owns one DialogBox and one ChoiceBox. Wild encounters: after each completed step (not a warp) `checkStep()` rolls; on success the player halts on that tile and a wild battle starts (no rolls while `debugFlags.encounters` is off). Enemy NPCs: after each player step (and NPC step while the player stands) `findSpotter()` checks the `ready` enemies; a spotted player halts, input locks, a "!" shows 600 ms (`NPC_ALERT_MS`), the NPC walks up (its path reserved), both face each other, it says `dialogue`, then an `npc` battle against a fresh copy of its team (`npcId` passed). A facing a `ready` enemy starts the same sequence; a `cooldown` one says `defeatDialogue`. After a win it says `defeatDialogue`; cooldowns refresh on map entry and every step. After a loss it restarts at the respawn point with "Te has despertado en la oficina..." (`messages` in the start data). A `healer` heals team and storage, says "Tus flexymon están como nuevos." + its dialogue and emits `save:request` (`healer`); a `storage` / `shopkeeper` NPC opens the Storage / Shop scene after its dialogue, launched over this scene, which pauses (locked) until `overlay:closed`. Start while standing still and free opens the Menu scene the same way (game time stops); every map entry asks for an auto-save (`save:request` `map`). The minimap (`src/ui/minimap.ts`) top left outdoors, toggled with M / touch Mapa (not while the debug menu is open). A facing a car warp (`car` property): the office car shows `car.json › lockedDialogue` unless the bag holds both items of an unlock pair (never consumed); otherwise (and always for the Murcia car) "¿Subir al Audi?" stays in the DialogBox (`hold`) with Sí / No beside it (B = No); Sí fades out, launches Car and sleeps; on `car:ended` it restarts at the car.json arrival point, fading in. A facing the mystery door (an action warp with `portal = true`): `story.json › door`, then "¿Cruzar la puerta?" Sí / No (B = No); Sí emits `save:request` (`portal`), fades out and starts Story in place of this scene (`enterStory()`, also an E2E hook). |
| `Story` | `story-scene.ts` | The story realm's board (*Modo historia*), started by the Overworld's mystery door: draws `story.json › map` with `drawZoneMap`, the player on the story state's stop, the bosses that stand there now (beaten ones and the next one; later ones hidden) and a flag on every beaten stop; the camera follows the player; a bottom panel names the stop ("Parada n/7 · {place}") and what A does. Setup by stage: `welcome` lines, then StoryTeam (required), then the story Shop, then the map. Moving: the direction of the path's first step towards the next / previous stop walks the whole leg (`walkTiles`), input waits until it arrives (story saved quietly); towards a closed stop "Primero tienes que vencer a {name}.". A at the office: menu Cambiar equipo (StoryTeam) / Tienda (Shop `{ story: true }`) / Volver al juego (save, fade, restart the Overworld where the main game's player stands) / Reiniciar historia (Sí / No → `restartStory`, setup again) / Salir. A at a boss: the ready one says `dialogue` and a battle starts (flash, Battle with `story: { at }` against `bossTeam`, this scene sleeps); a beaten one says `defeatDialogue` and offers "¿Revancha?". On `battle:ended`: fade in; a first win shows `defeatDialogue` (+ `story.json › victory` after the last) and redraws the bosses; a loss says "Tu equipo se ha recuperado…". Overlays pause this scene until `overlay:closed`. Emits `story:entered` (main.ts plays `story.json › music`). Rules: `src/logic/story.ts`. |
| `StoryTeam` | `story-team-scene.ts` | The story team picker, over the paused Story. Start with `{ required? }`. Left: every species (icon, name, "1º"… on the picks; the rest greyed while the team is full); right: the species under the cursor (front sprite, stats, attacks). A picks / unpicks (`togglePick`); the last pick shows the team on the left and "¿Vas con este equipo?" Sí / No; Sí → `pickTeam`, saves the story, `overlay:closed`. B leaves without changes, except on the first (required) pick, where it does nothing. Starts with the current team picked. |
| `Battle` | `battle-scene.ts` | The battle screen, driven by `src/logic/battle.ts`. Start with `{ kind, opponent[], seed? }`; the team and bag come from `src/core/session.ts`. Plays each round's `events[]` through `viewSteps()` (`src/logic/battle-view.ts`): messages in the DialogBox (A to go on), attack sounds, shake/flash, HP bars over 400 ms, faint/switch. Menus: main 2 × 2 (Huir greyed vs NPCs), attacks + accuracy, team (fainted greyed); B goes back from every sub-menu (the forced switch after a faint cannot be cancelled); Objeto: battle items with "x{n}" (`itemMenu`), then a target for heal / revive items (`itemTargetMenu`); using one plays the round (consumed, the opponent acts). After a wild win: "¿Te llevas a {name}?" + ChoiceBox Llevármelo / Dejarlo (B = Dejarlo); taking it runs `takeFlexymon()` and shows the added / storage / merged / maxed message. At the end it writes the team back, fades out, asks for an auto-save (`save:request` `battle`), emits `battle:ended` and stops. With `story: { at }` (a story boss) the team and a copy of the loadout come from the story state, and the end runs `afterStoryBattle` (heal; a first win counts the boss), saves the story slot and skips rewards, the Losing rule and the main-game save. With `npcId`, a win pays the reward (`winAgainstNpc`) and shows "¡Has ganado {n} €!"; any loss applies the Losing rule (`loseBattle`) to the game state. Backdrop (`drawBackdrop`): pale sky-to-grass bands and a rimmed grassy platform under each flexymon; panels use `src/ui/frame.ts`. |
| `Car` | `car-scene.ts` | The Audi trip. The Overworld launches it with `{ to: 'murcia' | 'valencia' }` and sleeps. A side-scrolling road scene drawn from shapes (sky, far mountains, bushes and road scrolling at different speeds, white speed lines, the car bobbing, the camera shaking every 900 ms) with `car.json › sprite` (side view, nose right; flipped for the way back) and `car.json › sound` looped at the effects volume. Lasts `CAR_TRIP_MS` (5 s); from `CAR_SKIP_AFTER_MS` (1 s) any button (A, B, Start, Mapa, a direction) skips it. Then it fades out, emits `car:ended { to }` and stops; the Overworld restarts at `car.json › toMurcia` / `toValencia`. Rules: `src/logic/car.ts`. |
| `Shop` | `shop-scene.ts` | The shop, over the paused overworld; start with `{ shopId }`. Money box, Comprar / Vender / Salir; lists from `src/logic/shop.ts` (`buyRows`, `sellRows`: icons, prices, greyed unsellable items); quantity picker 1 … limit (Up/Down ±1, Right/Left ±`QTY_BIG_STEP`) with the total; "¿Compras / Vendes {n} × {item} por {total} €?" Sí / No; `buy` / `sell` from `economy.ts` write money and bag to the game state. B goes back a step; leaving emits `save:request` (`shop`) and `overlay:closed`. With `{ story: true }` it is the story shop: `story.json › shop`, the story budget ("Presupuesto: {n} €") and loadout, buy-back at the full price (`refundPrice`), and leaving saves the story slot instead. |
| `Storage` | `storage-scene.ts` | The storage box, over the paused overworld. Columns Equipo (n/slots) and Caja (n) with icon, name, HP; Up/Down move, Left/Right switch column, A moves to the other column (`boxMove`: never leaves the team empty, never over its slots; refusals show a message); long columns scroll. B emits `overlay:closed` and stops. |
| `Menu` | `menu-scene.ts` | The Start menu, over the paused overworld: Equipo, Mochila, Mapa, Opciones, Cerrar on the right. Equipo: team list (icon, name, PS) → detail page (front sprite, the four stats as "value (base + bonus)", attacks with accuracy) with Mover (pick the new position; first = first into battle) / Volver. Mochila: bag with "x{n}" and the description at the bottom; field items (heal / revive → pick a team member, greyed when useless; Regleta at once) run `useFieldItem`; other items greyed; empty bag says so. Mapa: outdoors toggles the minimap ("Minimapa activado." / "Minimapa desactivado.", visible at once), indoors "Aquí dentro no hay mapa.". Opciones: Música / Efectos 0–10, Texto lenta / normal / rápida; Left/Right change at once (stored, applied live). B goes back a step; B, Start or Cerrar on the main list closes: `save:request` (`menu`), `overlay:closed`. Rules: `src/logic/menu.ts`, `src/logic/options.ts`. |
| `Hud` | `hud-scene.ts` | Always-on layer, launched by Preload, under Debug in the scene list: shows a small "Guardando…" box at the top centre (it was top right, under the touch Start / Mapa buttons) for 600 ms (`SAVE_ICON_MS`) on every `save:done`. Reads no input. The `currentScene()` hook ignores it. |
| `Debug` | `debug-scene.ts` | Dev-only overlay (dev builds or `?debug=1`). F2 cycles hidden → info (map, tile, FPS) → teleport menu (up/down, A = go to the zone's `default` spawn, B = close; the story board too, as a plain map; below the zones one "Combate: {name}" entry per flexymon starts a wild battle; last, under "Opciones", "Encuentros: sí/no" toggles wild encounters and keeps the menu open; "NPC: todos listos" sets every enemy NPC `ready`; "Borrar partida", first under Opciones: `debug:delete_save` → `main.ts` stops auto-saving, deletes `flexymon.save.v1` and `flexymon.story.v1` and reloads the page) → hidden. The info lines show the euros. Groups "Dar" ("Dar 1000 €", then "Dar: {item}" per item; the menu stays open) and "Datos" ("Recargar datos": reload and validate every JSON and map without a page reload; on success the game state is checked against the new data, new sprites / sounds load and `debug:reloaded` makes the Overworld rebuild the map where the player stands; on error the old data stays and the overlay shows "Error al recargar (n)" with the first errors) between the battles and Opciones. The menu draws a 14-line window that follows the cursor (the hook `debugLines()` still returns every line). Last in the scene list so it draws on top. |

Scenes read controls only through `src/core/input.ts` (Phaser's keyboard plugin is turned off in `src/config/game.ts`).

Interface sounds (`src/core/ui-sound.ts`): menus and choice boxes play `cursor` / `confirm` / `cancel` (and `error` on a greyed option); Title, Name, Menu (Opciones page, Start to close), Story (a closed stop), Shop (picker, refusals, `money` on a deal), Storage (moves, refusals) and Battle (`money` on an enemy's reward) add their own.
